# Open Questions

Central register of unresolved questions. A question lands here whenever a gap cannot be closed in the session that finds it: a source read only in part, a claim that lacks a primary source, an ambiguity only the operator can resolve, or speculation from `memory/research/` awaiting confirmation. The rule this file operationalises: never invent a claim to fill a gap, flag it here instead. In-file `## Open Questions` sections in raw and research extracts acknowledge the gap at the point of occurrence; this register is where those gaps are tracked to resolution.

Not for decisions. A choice awaiting the wave is a `#decision-pending` task in [active-tasks.md](active-tasks.md) under the Decision Pending protocol ([task-system.md](task-system.md)). A question belongs here when the missing piece is information, not commitment.

## Entry format

```markdown
### {{Short question title}}

- **Question:** {{The precise question, one or two sentences}}
- **Raised:** {{YYYY-MM-DD}} in {{[[source file, session, or operation]]}}
- **Entity:** {{#entity tag}}
- **Blocks:** {{What cannot proceed or be asserted until resolved, or "nothing"}}
- **Resolution path:** {{primary source to read | research to run | operator to answer | external party}}
```

On resolution: move the entry to Resolved, add **Resolved:** date and **Answer:** with the source, and propagate the answer to every file that carried the gap before closing the operation.
